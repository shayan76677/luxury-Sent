Composed slowly, bottled in small batches

Every Maison Veyra fragrance begins as a single accord, tested on skin for weeks before we add anything else. We use natural extracts wherever they perform best, and fine synthetics where they keep a scent stable and kind to skin.

Each batch rests for six weeks before filtering, so the notes have time to blend instead of sitting side by side.
